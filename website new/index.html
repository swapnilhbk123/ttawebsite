TTA Group Adventure Agency is your local expert in the Caucasus and South-East Asia, bridging the gap between global partners and seamless travel execution.

Company Profile

The DMC Advantage

Your Local Experts.

A Destination Management Company (DMC) is a professional service firm that functions as a local expert for a specific region. While travel agents focus on selling individual components like flights, a DMC manages the actual execution of the trip on the ground.

At TTA Group, we act as the local office for international travel partners, ensuring that all logistics within our destinations are handled professionally.

Our Model

B2B Synergy.

We primarily work in a Business-to-Business capacity. International tour operators partner with us because they need a reliable local entity to oversee their clients’ safety and comfort.

Core Functions

Ground Logistics

Coordinating airport pickups, private vehicle transfers with professional drivers, and inter-city transport.

Local Procurement Program Implementation

Transforming concepts into functional schedules, managing fees, and timing group activities.

The Value We Provide

Local Knowledge: Efficient routes and dependable local vendors.

Accountability: Real-time resolution for delays or local issues.

Standardization: Professional quality control across all services.
