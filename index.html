Business Profile

The DMC Identity

At TTA Group Adventure Agency, we act as the local office for international travel partners. While travel agents focus on individual components, we manage the actual execution of the trip on the ground. We bridge the gap between a general travel concept and a predictable, high-quality schedule.

B2B Capacity

We primarily work in a B2B capacity. International tour operators partner with us for a reliable local entity to oversee their clients’ safety and comfort.

1. Booking & Payments

All bookings are subject to availability. Prices are quoted in local currencies of the destinations and include applicable taxes unless stated otherwise. A booking is confirmed only after successful payment and receipt of a confirmation email.

2. What Your Price Includes

Inclusions are listed on each tour page and may cover accommodation, transport, activities, and meals as specified. Anything not explicitly mentioned as included is considered excluded.

3. Cancellations & Refunds

Cancel the booking between 30 to 60 days prior to date of journey, and receive full refund. Between 15 to 30 days, 50% amount of the total tour cost will be deducted. Between 0 to 15 days, no refunds would be issued.

4. Changes by You

Date or name changes are subject to availability and any fare differences or supplier fees. We’ll do our best to help but can’t guarantee changes close to departure.

5. Changes by Us / Force Majeure

We may modify itineraries due to weather, safety, government advisories, or operational reasons. In such cases, we will offer an equivalent alternative or credit where reasonable.
