THE DMC
ADVANTAGE.

TTA Group Adventure Agency is a professional service firm that functions as a local expert for a specific region. While travel agents focus on selling individual components like flights, a DMC manages the actual execution of the trip on the ground.

At TTA Group Adventure Agency, we act as the local office for international travel partners, ensuring that all logistics and services within our destinations are handled reliably and professionally.

GROUND LOGISTICS

We coordinate all physical movement, including airport pickups, private vehicle transfers with professional drivers, and inter-city transport.

LOCAL PROCUREMENT

We use our local relationships to vet and book hotels, restaurants, and tour guides, ensuring they meet specific quality standards.

PROGRAM IMPLEMENTATION

We take a general travel concept and turn it into a functional day-by-day schedule, managing everything from entrance fees to group activity timing.

Compliance & Policy

B2B TERMS & CONDITIONS.

01. Booking Confirmation

Services are confirmed only upon receipt of advance payment and issuance of an official TTA Group Adventure Agency digital voucher.

02. B2B Pricing

All rates are Nett and non-commissionable. Rates include local taxes but are subject to revision if government taxes change.

03. Cancellation Policy

30-60 Days: 100% Refund. 15-30 Days: 50% Refund. Less than 15 Days: No refund/100% cancellation charges apply.

04. Amendment Fees

First amendment is free; subsequent changes to confirmed itineraries incur a service fee of $25 per change.

05. Vehicle Usage

Standard vehicles are provided for point-to-point transfers and listed sightseeing only (8 hours/80km per day typical).

06. Child Policy

Children under 2 years are FOC (sharing bed). Children 2-11 years are charged at 75% of adult rate with an extra bed.

07. Hotel Check-in

Standard check-in is 14:00 and check-out is 11:00. Early check-in is subject to availability and may be charged extra.

08. Force Majeure

TTA Group Adventure Agency is not liable for delays or cancellations caused by weather, strikes, or political unrest.

09. No-Show Policy

No-shows for transfers or tours are non-refundable. Drivers will wait a maximum of 30 minutes at pickup points.

10. Luggage Policy

Standard allowance is 1 medium suitcase and 1 handbag per person. Oversized luggage requires a vehicle upgrade at cost.

11. Insurance

It is mandatory for all guests to have valid international travel insurance. TTA Group Adventure Agency is not liable for medical emergencies.

12. Snowfall Clause

In regions requiring 4x4 vehicles due to heavy snow, the cost difference is borne by the client at the time of service.

13. Payments

Final payment must be cleared 21 days prior to arrival. Failure to pay may result in automatic cancellation.

14. Guide Services

Driver-cum-guides provide basic info. For historical context, a licensed professional guide must be pre-booked.

15. Legal Jurisdiction

Any disputes or claims arising shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the company’s registered headquarters.
